- **Open Qualifier**: December 5 ‑ December 6, 2026
- **Main Events**: December 10 ‑ December 13, 2026

- **TO**: iKin Esports
- **Online Servers**: Hangzhou
- **Registration Window**: December 1 ‑ December 3, 2026

> Teams would need regist on 5E Client tournaments page.

If fewer than 16 teams remain registered after the extended window, the event will be cancelled for failing to meet minimum competition requirements.

## Format
### Open Qualifier (Online, December 2026)
- 64‑team single‑elimination bracket; all matches are Best‑of‑3 (BO3)

| Date | Item | 
|---|---|
| Dec 5 | Round‑of‑64 and Round‑of‑32 | 
| Dec 6 | Round‑of‑16 and Round‑of‑8 | 

> Round-of-8 matchs winner advance to Main Events

### Main Events (Online, December 2026)
- 4‑team double‑elimination bracket; all matches are Best‑of‑3 (BO3)

| Date | Item | 
|---|---|
| Dec 10 | Opening Matchs | 
| Dec 11 | UB Final and LB Round 1 | 
| Dec 12 | LB Final | 
| Dec 13 | Grand Final |
