# IKIN Master 2026

## Event Dates
- **Open Qualifier**: December 7 ‑ December 9, 2026
- **Main Events**: December 14 ‑ December 17, 2026

- **TO**: iKin Esports
- **Online Servers**: Hangzhou
- **Registration Window**: December 1 ‑ December 5, 2026

> Teams would need regist on 5E Client tournaments page.

- **Team Cap**: Maximum of **64 registered teams**

## Supplementary Provisions

If fewer than 16 teams remain registered after the registration window, the event will be cancelled for failing to meet minimum competition requirements.

## Format
### Open Qualifier (Online, December 2026)
- 64‑team single‑elimination bracket; all matches are Best‑of‑3 (BO3)

| Date | Item | 
|---|---|
| Dec 7 | Round‑of‑64 and Round‑of‑32 | 
| Dec 8 | Round‑of‑16 |
| Dec 9 | Round‑of‑8 | 

> Round-of-8 matchs winner advance to Main Events

### Main Events (Online, December 2026)
- 4‑team double‑elimination bracket; all matches are Best‑of‑3 (BO3)

| Date | Item | 
|---|---|
| Dec 14 | Opening Matchs | 
| Dec 15 | UB Final and LB Round 1 | 
| Dec 16 | LB Final | 
| Dec 17 | Grand Final |
